# Providence Auto — Writing Angle

How to write Providence copy so it carries the position in
`brand-position.md`. This is the sentence-level companion to that document:
the position says *what we claim*, this says *how a sentence has to be built
for the claim to survive*.

Governs every customer-facing surface — marketing and campaign pages, car
pages, blog, news, sales profiles, emails, form microcopy, FAQ answers.

---

## 1. The one rule

**The customer is the subject of the sentence. Providence is what makes the
sentence possible.**

> ✗ *We buy your car at Japanese auction and deliver it to your door.*
> ✓ *Now you can buy your dream car direct from Japanese auction.*

Both sentences describe the same transaction. The first makes the reader a
passenger; the second makes them the buyer and us the reason they can be.
Every rewrite in this document is a version of that swap.

**The test:** find the main verb. Who is doing it? If the answer is "we" more
often than "you" across a section, the section is off-position.

This is not a ban on "we". Providence does real things and should say so —
*we inspect it before your money moves*, *we bid to your instruction*. The
rule is about **who the outcome belongs to**. We act; you buy.

## 2. Never sell the removal of effort

The old copy sold relief: *handled, managed, taken care of, so you don't have
to*. We now sell **capability and access**. The distinction matters because
relief is a promise we can't fully keep (we ship CNF to port; we clear
customs directly in Ireland only) and capability is one we can.

| Banned | Why | Use instead |
|---|---|---|
| "so you don't have to" | Sells ignorance as a benefit; inverts the position exactly. | "so you can", "so you know exactly what you're buying" |
| "we handle everything" / "we take care of it all" | Untrue at the destination port outside Ireland. | "we prepare the file, and support you through clearance" |
| "leave it to us" / "sit back" | Passenger framing. | "you decide, on real numbers" |
| "hassle-free" / "zero paperwork for you" | The paperwork exists. Denying it reads as a sales line and loses trust the first time a form appears. | "the paperwork, prepared and explained" |
| "door-to-door" (as a blanket claim) | We quote CNF to the destination port. See `business-context.md` §4.2. | "to your port, with clearance support" — or name the specific service where it is real (Ireland NCTS, UK NOVA/DVLA) |
| "we clear customs for you" (outside Ireland) | True in Ireland only. | "clearance support at [port]" |
| "full service" / "white glove" / "concierge" | The retired proposition. | "direct access, with the tools to use it" |

## 3. The words that carry the position

Reach for these. They are load-bearing, not decoration.

- **direct / direct from source / at source** — the access pillar.
- **you can now** — the unlock. Signals a door that used to be shut.
- **see / read / check / verify** — the tools pillar. Prefer these to
  "trust". *You see the auction sheet* beats *you can trust our grading*.
- **before you commit / before your money moves** — the sequencing that makes
  every other claim safe.
- **the same … the trade uses** — the access idea made concrete: the same
  auction floor, the same condition grade, the same inspection data dealers
  have relied on for decades.
- **support / prepare / confirm / explain** — the documentation pillar. Honest
  verbs for what we do with paperwork.
- **your call / your decision / you choose** — agency, returned explicitly.

## 4. Rewrite patterns

Worked examples. The left column is real copy that was on the site.

| Before | After | What moved |
|---|---|---|
| "We Buy Your Car at Japanese Auction and Deliver It to Your Door." | "Now You Can Buy Your Dream Car Direct from Japanese Auction." | Subject: we → you. |
| "We navigate complex international tax codes and import duties so you don't have to." | "We map the tax and duty position for your country before you commit, so you can see the real number and decide on it." | Relief → visibility. Adds the sequencing. |
| "Your Country's Rules, Handled" | "Your Country's Rules, Confirmed First" | "Handled" (we absorb it) → "confirmed" (you know it). |
| "Door-to-Door Logistics" | "Shipping and Paperwork Support" | Fixes the CNF over-claim and names the real service. |
| "Our own teams in eight countries handle every form, marine insurance policy and freight mile for you. Zero friction." | "Our own teams prepare every form, arrange the marine cover and book the freight — and show you each step as it happens." | "for you / zero friction" → "prepare and show you". Also corrects eight → the actual claim. |
| "We handle the entire claims process—zero paperwork for you." | "We run the claim with the insurer and keep you updated — you don't chase it alone." | Denying paperwork → naming who does the chasing. |

## 5. B2B and B2C open the same way

The first thing a dealer reads and the first thing a private buyer reads is
the **same sentence**: you can source cars directly from the market they come
from and ship them to your own country, and we give you the access, tools and
documentation support to do it. See `brand-position.md` §5.

Only the *second* line differs:

- **B2C** adds the price advantage of cutting the dealership out — you buy at
  the price a dealer buys at, and that margin stays with you.
- **B2B** adds stock you don't have to floor-plan — a global inventory sold
  under your own brand, without owning it.

Do not open B2C with relief ("we'll handle it") or B2B with outsourcing
("leave the sourcing to us"). Both are the retired concierge frame.

## 6. What does *not* change

Do not sand the edges off things that are already true and specific.

- **Concrete operational claims stay.** "Our own staff read the auction sheet
  in Japanese, stand in the hall when the car is bid on, physically inspect
  it afterwards." That is the people-on-the-ground pillar and it is exactly
  the kind of sentence this position wants.
- **The named, real services stay named.** Ireland's NCTS registration and
  the UK's NOVA/DVLA handling are genuine done-for-you services. Say so
  plainly. The rule bans *blanket* concierge claims, not accurate specific
  ones.
- **Numbers stay.** One landed price, the grade, the age rule, the ROI
  target. Precision is the brand.

## 7. Interaction with the existing rules

This document adds to, and does not override:

- **Heading language** (`CLAUDE.md`) — one heading says the whole thing; no
  eyebrow label restating the H2 beneath it.
- **No filler copy** — no sub-line that only tells the reader how to feel
  about the section above it. A line earns its place by carrying a fact.
- **News voice** (`news-editorial-playbook.md` §4) — "The Landed Desk" is a
  narrower register than marketing copy: numerate, anti-hype, no selling in
  the article body. Where the two overlap, the news playbook wins on
  `/latest-news`.
- **Never invent a tax figure; never forecast a currency.** The empowerment
  angle raises the stakes here rather than lowering them — copy that hands
  the reader numbers to decide on must have those numbers right.

## 8. Pre-publish check

**Read `business-context.md`, `brand-position.md` and this document before
writing or changing any public copy, any outbound message, or building
anything that carries copy.** Every time — see `brand-position.md` §11.1 for
why that is stated so bluntly.

Five questions for any customer-facing page:

1. **Subject test.** In the hero and the value props, is the reader doing the
   verbs, or watching us do them?
2. **Banned-phrase scan.** Any of §2 present? (`grep -i "so you don't have
   to\|handle everything\|door-to-door\|hassle-free"`)
3. **Pillar coverage.** Are all four pillars from `brand-position.md` §4
   present — access, tools, documentation, people?
4. **Scope honesty.** Does any claim promise clearance outside Ireland,
   delivery past the destination port, or that we pay a tax that is charged to
   the registered owner? (`business-context.md` §4)
5. **Opening line.** Do B2B and B2C open on the same proposition, with the
   audience-specific advantage second rather than first? (§5)

If the work shows a source-of-truth document is wrong or contradicts another,
do not edit it on your own authority: raise it, log it in `brand-position.md`
§11.3, and keep raising it until answered (`brand-position.md` §11.2).
